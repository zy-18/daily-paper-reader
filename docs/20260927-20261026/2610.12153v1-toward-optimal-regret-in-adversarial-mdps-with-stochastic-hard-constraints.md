## 摘要
我们研究在随机硬约束下具有对抗性损失的分幕式约束马尔可夫决策过程。具体而言，从具有裕度 $d$ 的已知严格可行策略出发，我们希望在每一幕均满足期望成本约束的同时获得最优遗憾。在此设定下，Stradi 等人（2025）表明，一种精心设计的混合规则可达到阶为 $\widetilde{\mathcal{O}}(\sqrt{T}/\min\{d,d^2\})$ 的遗憾。有趣的是，他们还针对同一设定给出了阶为 $Ω(\sqrt{T}/ρ)$ 的下界，其中 $ρ$ 是离线问题的 Slater 裕度，且可能远大于 $d$。在本工作中，我们基于他们的方法，获得对这些裕度的最优遗憾依赖。具体而言，我们提出 MA-OPS，一种将针对 Slater 裕度的乐观搜索与对所选策略的悲观评估相结合的算法，以安全地学习具有大可行性裕度的策略。随后，该策略被用于在每一幕满足约束的同时最小化遗憾。特别地，我们证明 MA-OPS 达到遗憾 $\widetilde{\mathcal{O}}(\sqrt{T}/ρ+ 1/(dρ))$。最后，我们给出一个匹配的下界，表明遗憾界中对 $T$、$d$、$ρ$ 的依赖在对数因子范围内是最优的。

## Abstract
We study episodic constrained Markov decision processes with adversarial losses under stochastic hard constraints. Specifically, starting from a known strictly feasible policy with margin $d$, we seek to obtain optimal regret while satisfying the expected cost constraints in every episode. In this setting, Stradi et al. (2025) show that a carefully designed mixing rule attains regret of order $\widetilde{\mathcal{O}}(\sqrt{T}/\min\{d,d^2\})$. Interestingly, they also provide a lower bound of order $Ω(\sqrt{T}/ρ)$ for the same setting, where $ρ$ is the Slater margin of the offline problem and can be much larger than $d$. In this work, we build on their approach to obtain optimal regret dependence on these margins. Specifically, we propose MA-OPS, an algorithm that combines an optimistic search for the Slater margin with a pessimistic evaluation of the selected policies to safely learn a policy with a large feasibility margin. This policy is then used to minimize regret while satisfying the constraints at every episode. In particular, we show that MA-OPS attains regret $\widetilde{\mathcal{O}}(\sqrt{T}/ρ+ 1/(dρ))$. Finally, we provide a matching lower bound, showing that the dependence on $T$, $d$, $ρ$ in the regret bound is optimal up to logarithmic factors.
