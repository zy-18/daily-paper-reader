## 摘要
在线强化学习（RL）使驾驶策略能够适应不断变化的部署条件，但要求在整个训练过程中进行安全探索。仅靠安全过滤并不能确保策略稳定改进或降低对纠正的依赖。我们提出安全在线自适应精炼（SOAR-Flow），这是一个用于对离线初始化的基于流的驾驶策略进行安全且稳定在线微调的框架。控制屏障函数（CBF）—模型预测控制（MPC）滤波器在执行前纠正可能不安全的动作。为支持稳定的策略更新，我们引入基于校准Q学习（Cal-QL）的高斯回报感知价值校准，利用记录的行为回报和预测的回报变异性来平滑保守评论家更新并缓解策略退化。成对纠正学习教授可行动作调整，而干预至目标预测器鼓励做出需要更少未来纠正的决策。其预测还调节引导强度，使恢复监督随着预测依赖降低而减弱。结合逐渐减小的校准正则化，这支持在保持硬执行约束的同时持续改进任务。我们分析了控制步安全执行的条件以及辅助学习影响逐渐减弱的条件。我们在仿真和无人地面车辆（UGV）上评估SOAR-Flow。结果表明，在受保护的在线微调过程中，驾驶性能得到提升且对安全纠正的依赖降低。演示视频和项目概览见 https://lzqw.github.io/SOAR-Flow/。

## Abstract
Online reinforcement learning (RL) enables driving policies to adapt to changing deployment conditions, but requires safe exploration throughout training. Safety filtering alone does not ensure stable policy improvement or reduced dependence on corrections. We propose Safe Online Adaptive Refinement (SOAR-Flow), a framework for safe and stable online fine-tuning of offline-initialized Flow-based driving policies. A control barrier function (CBF)--model predictive control (MPC) filter corrects potentially unsafe actions before execution. To support stable policy updates, we introduce Gaussian-return-aware value calibration based on calibrated Q-learning (Cal-QL), using recorded behavior returns and predicted return variability to smooth conservative critic updates and mitigate policy degradation. Paired corrective learning teaches feasible action adjustments, while an intervention-to-go predictor encourages decisions requiring fewer future corrections. Its predictions also regulate guidance strength, allowing recovery supervision to weaken as predicted dependence decreases. Together with gradually reduced calibration regularization, this supports continued task improvement while preserving hard execution constraints. We analyze conditions for safe execution at control steps and diminishing auxiliary learning influences. We evaluate SOAR-Flow in simulation and on an uncrewed ground vehicle (UGV). The results demonstrate improved driving performance and reduced reliance on safety corrections during protected online fine-tuning. The demonstration video and project overview are available at https://lzqw.github.io/SOAR-Flow/.
