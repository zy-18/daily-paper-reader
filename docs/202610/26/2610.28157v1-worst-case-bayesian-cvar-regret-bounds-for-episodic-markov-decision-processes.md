## 摘要
我们研究在时间齐次、有限时域的分幕式马尔可夫决策过程中，累积伪遗憾的贝叶斯条件风险价值（CVaR）在所有先验上的最坏情况。针对UCBVI-BF的一个高概率保证给出了一个先验预测上界。通过一个三状态MDP并应用反向KL不等式，可得到一个下界。当状态数为常数时，这些界对时域、动作数和回合数具有相同的多项式依赖关系，但在风险水平上相差一个对数因子。

## Abstract
We study the worst-case, over priors, Bayesian conditional value-at-risk (CVaR) of cumulative pseudo-regret in time-homogeneous finite-horizon episodic Markov decision processes. A high-probability guarantee for UCBVI-BF yields a prior-predictive upper bound. A lower bound follows by a three-state MDP and applying a reverse-KL inequality. For a constant number of states, the bounds have the same polynomial dependence on horizon, actions, and episodes, with a logarithmic gap in the risk level.
