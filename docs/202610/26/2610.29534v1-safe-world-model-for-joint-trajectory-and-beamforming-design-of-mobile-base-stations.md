## 摘要
移动基站（MBSs），包括空中和地面平台，通过自适应定位提供灵活的无线覆盖。然而，联合优化其轨迹与数字波束成形具有挑战性，因为移动会改变信道条件和干扰，而波束成形决定产生的信号功率和干扰功率。这些耦合决策还需考虑当前移动如何影响未来位置以及在移动性和安全性约束下的通信性能。我们将该问题建模为约束马尔可夫决策过程，在移动性、禁入区规避和发射功率约束下最大化时间平均的SINR总和。为支持长期规划并减少无模型强化学习所需的大量环境交互，我们开发了一种基于SafeDreamer的世界模型框架，该框架从经验中学习潜在系统动力学，并通过想象轨迹优化联合轨迹与波束成形策略。保持可行性的动作映射和安全成本信号将运行约束纳入策略学习。仿真结果表明，与近端策略和深度Q网络基线相比，所提框架分别将时间平均的SINR总和平均提高7%和9%，同时实现更高的样本效率和更可靠的约束满足。

## Abstract
Mobile base stations (MBSs), including aerial and ground-based platforms, provide flexible wireless coverage through adaptive positioning. However, jointly optimizing their trajectories and digital beamforming is challenging because movement changes channel conditions and interference, while beamforming determines the resulting signal and interference powers. These coupled decisions also account for how current movements affect future positions and communication performance under mobility and safety constraints. We formulate this problem as a constrained Markov decision process that maximizes the time-averaged sum signal-to-interference-plus-noise ratio (SINR), subject to mobility, restricted-zone avoidance, and transmit-power constraints. To support long-term planning and reduce the extensive environment interactions required by model-free reinforcement learning, we develop a SafeDreamer-based world model framework that learns latent system dynamics from experience and optimizes a joint trajectory and beamforming policy through imagined trajectories. Feasibility-preserving action mappings and safety cost signals incorporate operational constraints into policy learning. Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed framework improves the time-averaged sum SINR by an average of 7\% and 9\% compared with proximal policy and deep Q-network baselines, respectively, while achieving higher sample efficiency and more reliable constraint satisfaction.
