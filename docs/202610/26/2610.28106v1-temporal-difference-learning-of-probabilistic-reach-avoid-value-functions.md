## 摘要
可达-规避集定义了在避开不安全区域的同时能够完成任务的状态，是在存在扰动和不确定性的环境中部署机器人以及进行运行时任务可行性监测的基础工具，可用于预测某一状态是否安全并将成功完成任务。经典的 Hamilton-Jacobi 可达性（HJR）及其近期的神经网络扩展（例如 Lipschitz 连续可达性学习、DeepReach）以对抗方式处理扰动，在有界集合中的最坏情况扰动下计算可达-规避集。这保证了安全性，但当扰动是随机的而非对抗性的时候，可能产生过于保守的集合，并且还要求扰动集合已知或预先指定。我们提出概率可达-规避值函数（PVF），一种适用于黑盒系统的自监督方法，用于在随机的、可能无界的扰动下将可达-规避集计算为具有特定可达-规避概率的状态。我们在复杂度递增的系统上（直至 Hopper 运动任务）将我们的方法与基于 HJR 的基线进行比较评估，并表明当扰动非对抗性时，我们的概率集合比对抗性 HJR 集合更不保守且更准确。

## Abstract
Reach-avoid sets, defining the states from which a task can be completed while avoiding unsafe regions, are fundamental tools for deploying robots in environments subject to disturbances and uncertainty, as well as for runtime task-feasibility monitoring, allowing prediction of whether a state is safe and will lead to successful task completion. Classical Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability (HJR) and its recent neural extensions (e.g., Lipschitz Continuous Reachability Learning, DeepReach) treat disturbances adversarially, computing the reach-avoid set under worst-case disturbances from a bounded set. This guarantees safety but can yield overly conservative sets when disturbances are stochastic rather than adversarial, and additionally requires the disturbance set to be known or specified in advance.   We propose Probabilistic reach-avoid Value Function (PVF), a self-supervised method, applicable to black-box systems, for computing reach-avoid sets as the states with a certain reach-avoid probability under stochastic, potentially unbounded disturbances. We evaluate our approach against HJR-based baselines on systems of increasing complexity, up to a Hopper locomotion task, and show that our probabilistic sets are less conservative and more accurate than adversarial HJR sets when disturbances are non-adversarial.
